ORIENTATION_ZONE_DIAGNOSTIC#

Python export: phenotypic.schema.ORIENTATION_ZONE_DIAGNOSTIC

Opt-in validation, comparator, and legacy orientation-zone columns.

The literal skeleton-ring diagnostics use the detected-object mask from the inferred inoculum boundary through the full object extent. The retained legacy field diagnostics use the structure-tensor field in symmetric-radius zones and include Radial (all tile pixels) and Mask variants. See MeasureOrientationZones for parameters and method.
