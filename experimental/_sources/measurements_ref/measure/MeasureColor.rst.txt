.. _measurement-op-measurecolor:

MeasureColor
============

Measure robust colorimetric statistics for each colony.

Parameters and usage: :py:class:`MeasureColor <phenotypic.measure.MeasureColor>`.

- **One row per:** detected colony
- **Written to:** ``deliverables/measurements.csv`` (every operation, with your metadata joined) and ``deliverables/measurements_by_feature/MeasureColor.csv``, each with a ``.parquet`` beside it
- **Columns:** 55

.. _measurement-info-colorhsv:

:doc:`ColorHSV </api_reference/api/phenotypic.schema.ColorHSV>`
---------------------------------------------------------------

.. list-table:: Metric family: **ColorHSV**
   :header-rows: 1
   :class: phenotypic-measurement-table

   * - Column
     - Description
     - Type
   * - ``ColorHSV_HueRobustMean``
     - Hue of the colony's robust center color, as a fraction of a full turn around the color wheel. It is the hue of the cone-embedded geometric-median robust center (circular-correct): the geometric median of the pixels at (S·V·cos 2πH, S·V·sin 2πH, V), which handles the wrap-around of hue at red.
     - :bdg-ref-primary:`Tier 2 · Descriptive trait <measurement-tiers>`
   * - ``ColorHSV_SaturationRobustMean``
     - Saturation of the colony's robust center color, from 0 for gray to 1 for a fully saturated color. It is the saturation of the cone-embedded geometric-median robust center.
     - :bdg-ref-primary:`Tier 2 · Descriptive trait <measurement-tiers>`
   * - ``ColorHSV_ValueRobustMean``
     - Brightness (HSV value, from 0 to 1) of the colony's robust center color. It is the value of the cone-embedded geometric-median robust center.
     - :bdg-ref-primary:`Tier 2 · Descriptive trait <measurement-tiers>`
   * - ``ColorHSV_HSVConeVariance``
     - How spread out the colony's pixel colors are in the HSV cone, as the sum of the variances of the three cone coordinates. It is the trace of the HSV cone-Cartesian covariance (single 3D HSV spread scalar); spread about the arithmetic mean of the cone coordinates (NOT about the reported RobustMean center).
     - :bdg-ref-primary:`Tier 2 · Descriptive trait <measurement-tiers>`
