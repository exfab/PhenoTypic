.. _measurement-op-measurebounds:

MeasureBounds
=============

Extract bounding box coordinates and centroids of detected colonies.

Parameters and usage: :py:class:`MeasureBounds <phenotypic.measure.MeasureBounds>`.

- **One row per:** detected colony
- **Written to:** ``deliverables/measurements.csv`` (every operation, with your metadata joined) and ``deliverables/measurements_by_feature/MeasureBounds.csv``, each with a ``.parquet`` beside it
- **Columns:** 10

.. _measurement-info-bbox:

:doc:`BBOX </api_reference/api/phenotypic.schema.BBOX>`
-------------------------------------------------------

.. list-table:: Metric family: **Bbox**
   :header-rows: 1
   :class: phenotypic-measurement-table

   * - Column
     - Description
     - Type
   * - ``Bbox_CenterRR``
     - Row coordinate of the colony's centroid, the mean position of its pixels (skimage ``centroid``).
     - :bdg-ref-muted:`Identity / design <measurement-classification>`
   * - ``Bbox_MinRR``
     - The smallest row coordinate of the bounding box.
     - :bdg-ref-muted:`Identity / design <measurement-classification>`
   * - ``Bbox_MaxRR``
     - The largest row coordinate of the bounding box. It is one past the colony's last pixel row (exclusive, as in Python slicing), so rows MinRR to MaxRR - 1 hold the colony and ``image[MinRR:MaxRR]`` covers it.
     - :bdg-ref-muted:`Identity / design <measurement-classification>`
   * - ``Bbox_CenterCC``
     - Column coordinate of the colony's centroid, the mean position of its pixels (skimage ``centroid``).
     - :bdg-ref-muted:`Identity / design <measurement-classification>`
   * - ``Bbox_MinCC``
     - The smallest column coordinate of the bounding box.
     - :bdg-ref-muted:`Identity / design <measurement-classification>`
   * - ``Bbox_MaxCC``
     - The largest column coordinate of the bounding box. It is one past the colony's last pixel column (exclusive, as in Python slicing), so columns MinCC to MaxCC - 1 hold the colony and ``image[:, MinCC:MaxCC]`` covers it.
     - :bdg-ref-muted:`Identity / design <measurement-classification>`
   * - ``Bbox_IntensityWeightedCenterRR``
     - The intensity-weighted center row coordinate of the object (skimage ``centroid_weighted``).
     - :bdg-ref-muted:`Identity / design <measurement-classification>`
   * - ``Bbox_IntensityWeightedCenterCC``
     - The intensity-weighted center column coordinate of the object (skimage ``centroid_weighted``).
     - :bdg-ref-muted:`Identity / design <measurement-classification>`
   * - ``Bbox_DistWeightedCenterRR``
     - Row coordinate of the colony's distance-weighted centroid: the mean position of its pixels, each weighted by its distance to the colony's edge. It is Σ dt·row / Σ dt over the Euclidean distance transform dt, and the plain centroid for a colony whose every pixel has a pixel of another colony among its eight neighbors (those pixels are zeroed before the transform, so its weights sum to 0). Robust to thin filamentous extensions that pull intensity-weighted centroids off-body.
     - :bdg-ref-muted:`Identity / design <measurement-classification>`
   * - ``Bbox_DistWeightedCenterCC``
     - Column coordinate of the colony's distance-weighted centroid: the mean position of its pixels, each weighted by its distance to the colony's edge. It is Σ dt·col / Σ dt over the Euclidean distance transform dt, and the plain centroid for a colony whose every pixel has a pixel of another colony among its eight neighbors (those pixels are zeroed before the transform, so its weights sum to 0). Robust to thin filamentous extensions that pull intensity-weighted centroids off-body.
     - :bdg-ref-muted:`Identity / design <measurement-classification>`
