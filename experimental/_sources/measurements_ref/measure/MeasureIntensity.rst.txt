.. _measurement-op-measureintensity:

MeasureIntensity
================

Measure grayscale intensity statistics of detected colonies.

Parameters and usage: :py:class:`MeasureIntensity <phenotypic.measure.MeasureIntensity>`.

- **One row per:** detected colony
- **Written to:** ``deliverables/measurements.csv`` (every operation, with your metadata joined) and ``deliverables/measurements_by_feature/MeasureIntensity.csv``, each with a ``.parquet`` beside it
- **Columns:** 12

.. _measurement-info-intensity:

:doc:`INTENSITY </api_reference/api/phenotypic.schema.INTENSITY>`
-----------------------------------------------------------------

.. versionchanged:: 0.21.0
   A single-channel (grayscale) integer image, such as an 8- or 16-bit
   grayscale PNG, TIFF or JPEG, is now normalised to ``[0, 1]`` when it is
   read, by dividing by its full-scale value (255 for 8-bit, 65535 for
   16-bit; another integer dtype takes the narrower of the two its values
   fit). An RGB image's grayscale was already on that scale, and RGB inputs
   are unchanged. These columns are now produced for such
   an image; earlier versions raised on it instead. Their values equal those
   of the same scan supplied as a float array in ``[0, 1]``.
   Detectors and enhancers that assume ``[0, 1]`` now see it, so the
   segmentation of such an image changes, and with it every column measured
   on its detected objects: ``Size_*``, ``Shape_*``, ``Bbox_*`` and the zone
   columns included.
   A single-channel run started before this change must be re-run with
   ``--overwrite``, not resumed. Resuming reuses the images it already
   finished, which were segmented and measured on raw integer counts.


.. list-table:: Metric family: **Intensity**
   :header-rows: 1
   :class: phenotypic-measurement-table

   * - Column
     - Description
     - Type
     - Categories
   * - ``Intensity_IntegratedIntensity``
     - Sum of the grayscale values of all the colony's pixels, so it grows with both the colony's area and how bright its pixels are.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
     - 
   * - ``Intensity_Density``
     - The colony's integrated intensity divided by its area in pixels: the mean grayscale value of its pixels, equal to MeanIntensity.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
     - 
   * - ``Intensity_ConvexDensity``
     - The colony's integrated intensity divided by the area of its convex hull, so indentations and gaps in the colony lower it relative to Density. The hull passes through pixel centers, so its area is smaller than the pixel count: slightly for a convex colony, where this value can slightly exceed Density, and substantially for an object of only a few pixels (a 2 x 2 pixel object has a hull area of 1, so this value is four times Density). It is NaN for a single pixel or a one-pixel-wide line, which has no hull.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
     - 
   * - ``Intensity_MinimumIntensity``
     - Lowest grayscale value among the colony's pixels.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
     - 
   * - ``Intensity_MaximumIntensity``
     - Highest grayscale value among the colony's pixels.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
     - 
   * - ``Intensity_MeanIntensity``
     - Average grayscale value of the colony's pixels, on the gray layer's scale from 0 for black to 1 for the brightest value the image's pixels can hold. For an RGB scan the gray layer is its luminance, so 1 is white; for a single-channel scan 1 is the 8- or 16-bit maximum.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
     - :bdg-ref-info-line:`Starting Metrics <measurement-category-startingmetrics>`
   * - ``Intensity_MedianIntensity``
     - Middle grayscale value of the colony's pixels: half of them are at or below it, so a few very bright or very dark pixels move it less than they move the mean.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
     - 
   * - ``Intensity_StandardDeviationIntensity``
     - How much the grayscale values of the colony's pixels vary around their mean, as their standard deviation. It is the population standard deviation, dividing by the pixel count n.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
     - 
   * - ``Intensity_CoefficientVarianceIntensity``
     - How much the colony's grayscale values vary relative to their mean, as the standard deviation divided by the mean. The ratio is then multiplied by (1 + 1/n), where n is the colony's pixel count.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
     - 
   * - ``Intensity_LowerQuartileIntensity``
     - Grayscale value that about a quarter of the colony's pixels are at or below. It is the 25th percentile, linearly interpolated.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
     - 
   * - ``Intensity_UpperQuartileIntensity``
     - Grayscale value that about three quarters of the colony's pixels are at or below. It is the 75th percentile, linearly interpolated.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
     - 
   * - ``Intensity_InterquartileRangeIntensity``
     - Spread of the middle half of the colony's grayscale values, from the lower to the upper quartile. It is UpperQuartileIntensity minus LowerQuartileIntensity.
     - :bdg-ref-success:`Tier 1 · Direct phenotype <measurement-tiers>`
