ORIENTATION_ZONES
=================

Python object: ``phenotypic.schema._orientation_zones.ORIENTATION_ZONES``

Per-zone hyphal orientation traits (concentration, turning, coherence).

Computed from the structure-tensor orientation field over a mask-free tile,
aggregated coherence-weighted over radially-defined zones bounded by the
symmetric radius, in both a ``Radial`` (all tile pixels) and a raw ``Mask``
variant. See ``MeasureOrientationZones`` for parameters and method.

Dimensionless in [0, 1]; 1 = perfectly aligned hyphae, 0 = isotropic. NaN when the summed coherence over the selector is ~0 or the zone has zero width.
