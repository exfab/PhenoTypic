.. _measurement-op-measurebounds:

MeasureBounds
=============

Extract bounding box coordinates and centroids of detected colonies.

Parameters and usage: :py:class:`MeasureBounds <phenotypic.measure.MeasureBounds>`.

- **One row per:** detected colony
- **Written to:** ``deliverables/measurements.csv`` (every operation, with your metadata joined) and ``deliverables/measurements_by_feature/MeasureBounds.csv``, each with a ``.parquet`` beside it
- **Columns:** 10
