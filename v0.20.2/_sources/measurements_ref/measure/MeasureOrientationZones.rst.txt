MeasureOrientationZones
=======================

Measure absolute and radial-relative hyphal orientation by growth zone.

Parameters and usage: :py:class:`MeasureOrientationZones <phenotypic.measure.MeasureOrientationZones>`.

- **One row per:** detected colony
- **Written to:** ``deliverables/measurements.csv`` (every operation, with your metadata joined) and ``deliverables/measurements_by_feature/MeasureOrientationZones.csv``, each with a ``.parquet`` beside it
- **Columns:** 92
