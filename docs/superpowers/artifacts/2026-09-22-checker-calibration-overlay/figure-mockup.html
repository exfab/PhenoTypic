Open questions

What I need you to decide

  1. Q1 · Rendering stack. ColorCheckerProfile.report() already renders with plotly, through PhtPlot and the @figure sections. Matching it gives hover values and one look across both entry points. matplotlib gives a static PNG/PDF that fits a lab notebook. Recommendation: plotly, to match.
  2. Q2 · Entry point. The overlay needs the pixels, and the operation doesn't keep the image. The options are op.report(image) after apply, or an opt-in flag that stores the ROI crops during apply. Recommendation: op.report(image). Nothing is stored on the operation or serialised.
  3. Q3 · Labels. Is the full patch name, measured | reference swatch pair, and ΔE00 before → after the right content? On a real 2150 × 340 px band, the labels will sit beside the ROI, as here.
  4. Q4 · Corrected crops (B′). Keep them, drop them, or make them a toggle?
  5. Q5 · ΔE00 colour bands. Proposed: ≤ 2 good, 2–5 fair, > 5 poor. Should they be fixed, or configurable?
  6. Q6 · Skipped or refused frames. Should the figure still render, showing the overlays with the refusal reasons and no after-values?
  7. Q7 · Batch use. Add a flag to write this figure per image in CLI runs, e.g. into deliverables/qc/? Or keep it notebook-only for now?
