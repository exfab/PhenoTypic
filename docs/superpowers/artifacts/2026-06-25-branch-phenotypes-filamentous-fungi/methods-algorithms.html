Methods & algorithms

These are the extraction, tracing, enhancement, and segmentation techniques — not the phenotypes — aggregated from the four analog fields into one place. It is the actionable toolkit for turning dense, faint, overlapping hyphae on textured agar into a measurable network: a front-end that denoises and enhances, a tracer that threads crossings, a learned segmenter, a junction resolver, and a validation gate. This page is a view — nothing here is new; each method links back to the source artifact it was drawn from.

1 · Enhancement & denoising (front-end)

Before any threshold: lift faint thin hyphae above the per-pixel noise floor and strip agar texture. The governing principle is integration beats thresholding — accumulate sub-noise signal over scales, lines, or orientation so SNR grows as √(pixels integrated).

Hessian vesselness / tubeness (Frangi/Sato)

Enhance

The multiscale second-derivative filter behind essentially all curvilinear enhancement. At each scale σ it eigen-decomposes the image Hessian: a tube gives one near-zero eigenvalue λ1 (along it) and one large-negative λ2 (across it). The eigenvalue-ratio "vesselness" rewards tubes and suppresses blobs and isotropic noise — recommended independently by the vascular, astronomy, and mycology pipelines.

FromVascularAstronomyNeuro
→ Fungiadd a Frangi/Sato vesselness channel to assemble_composite_cost so faint thin hyphae survive thresholding — the top cost-surface upgrade for branch_pathfinding
SeeVascular · Astronomy
SourceFrangi et al. 1998; Sato et al. 1998; Cautun et al. 2013
Symbols
λ1
smaller-magnitude Hessian eigenvalue; ≈ 0 along a tube's axis
λ2
larger-magnitude Hessian eigenvalue; large-negative across a bright tube (|λ2| ≫ |λ1|)
σ
Gaussian filter scale (px); vesselness peaks where σ ≈ local tube radius

Matched filter / Radon line integration

Enhance

Cross-correlate the frame with a thin-line template matched to hyphal width (provably optimal SNR in Gaussian noise), or integrate along every candidate line with the Radon transform so a faint streak becomes a peak in an (angle, offset) accumulator. Both sum the whole feature's flux instead of trusting low-SNR pixels.

FromAstronomy
→ Fungian SNR-lifting line-filter front-end before branch_pathfinding thresholds — faint straight-hypha detection and enhancement
SeeAstronomy
SourceVio & Andreani 2016; Nir et al. 2018
Symbols
SNR
signal-to-noise ratio of the detection; grows as √(pixels integrated)
Radon
integral of image intensity along every candidate line (angle, offset)
template
line-shaped kernel matched to the signal, cross-correlated with the image

Starlet / curvelet wavelet denoising

Enhance

The isotropic undecimated (à trous) starlet wavelet models noise per scale and thresholds via a multiresolution support; curvelets add anisotropic atoms matched to thin curved edges. A pre-denoise that strips agar texture at the fine scale while preserving faint thin structure at the mid scale, then reconstructs.

FromAstronomy
→ Fungiprepend a denoise stage that strips agar texture while keeping thin curved hyphae — paired with the matched/Radon line filter as the front-end for retracing
SeeAstronomy
SourceStarck et al. 2007; Starck, Candès & Donoho 2002

Rolling Hough Transform (orientation in noise)

Enhance

Run a local Hough transform over a disk around every pixel, returning the probability P(linear) that the pixel belongs to a coherent line plus its orientation θ. It asks "is there local linear coherence?" not "is this pixel bright?", so faint coherent hyphae survive while incoherent agar speckle cancels.

FromAstronomy
→ Fungia noise-robust hyphal orientation channel for the cost surface — tip orientation and anisotropy at low SNR
SeeAstronomy
SourceClark, Peek & Putman 2014
Symbols
θ
local linear orientation returned at each pixel (degrees)
P(linear)
probability the pixel belongs to a coherent linear structure

2 · Tracing & skeletonization

Turn the enhanced field into a centreline graph. The recurring lesson: grow each strand as a single least-cost path so a crossing is traversed, not fused — and rank branches by persistence, not brightness, so texture never becomes a hallucinated branch.

A* / least-cost-path tracing (RootNav / SNT)

Trace

Make each pixel cheap-to-traverse where it looks hypha-like, then run A* (or multi-source Dijkstra) from tip to source. The path glides through a crossing because continuing straight costs less than the sharp turn onto the other strand. This is exactly what PhenoTypic's branch_pathfinding already does — the transfer is the refinements, not a new paradigm.

FromRootsNeuro
→ Fungithe backbone is already in place; the borrowed refinements are a vesselness cost term and direction-continuity edge pairing at junctions in branch_pathfinding
SeePlant roots · Neuroscience
SourcePound et al. 2013 (RootNav); Longair et al. 2011 (SNT)
Symbols
A*
heuristic least-cost pathfinding, run tip→source (SNT minimal-path tracer)
cost
per-pixel traversal cost — cheap where the pixel looks hypha-like

Flood-filling networks (dense tracing)

Trace

From a single seed, a recurrent field-of-view floods outward and re-segments one process at a time, marching past crossings instead of fusing them. The dense-tracing answer for a tangle where a skeleton-from-mask would merge every overlap into a false node.

FromNeuro
→ Fungia per-process re-segmentation route for dense mycelial tangles where mask-skeletons collapse crossings into false nodes

APP2 gray-weighted distance-tree pruning

Trace

Build an initial over-connected reconstruction as a gray-weighted distance tree (each pixel weighted by how hypha-like it is), then hierarchically prune spurs by leaf-to-root significance. The gray weighting keeps the tracer on the bright centreline; the pruning removes texture spurs without a brightness cut.

FromNeuro
→ Fungigray-weighted distance-tree tracing + hierarchical spur pruning as a post-trace cleanup for branch_pathfinding fragments
SeeNeuroscience
SourceXiao & Peng 2013 (APP2)

Steger subpixel curvilinear detector

Trace

Use the 2nd directional derivative (Hessian of Gaussian) to locate a line's centre to subpixel precision and recover its width w from the transverse profile — with an explicit line model that removes the asymmetry bias of naive ridge detection. The best tracer when a hypha is only a few pixels across.

FromAstronomy
→ Fungia subpixel hyphal centreline + width tracer at poor resolution — evaluate as the tracing front-end for branch_pathfinding
SeeAstronomy
SourceSteger 1998
Symbols
w
recovered line/hypha width from the transverse intensity profile (px)
2nd deriv.
Hessian of Gaussian; its extremum locates the subpixel centreline

DisPerSE Morse-persistence skeleton

Trace

Find filaments as ridge lines (ascending 1-manifolds) between density critical points, then prune by persistence — the density contrast from a feature's birth to its death. Persistence gives each branch a statistical significance instead of a brightness cut, so noise features are removed with confidence.

FromAstronomyNeuro
→ Fungia noise-robust skeletonizer whose per-branch persistence ranks candidate hyphae by significance — feeds a persistence-based path-quality filter in _path_quality
SeeAstronomy
SourceSousbie 2011
Symbols
persistence
density contrast from a feature's birth to its death; the pruning threshold = statistical significance
max / saddle
the density-maximum and saddle critical points anchoring a ridge

FilFinder medial-axis spine + width

Trace

Adaptive-threshold the frame to a mask, reduce to a 1-pixel medial axis, graph-prune to spines, then fit perpendicular intensity profiles (Gaussian/Plummer) to recover the FWHM width along each spine. The ISM-filament pipeline pattern: mask → skeleton → graph metrics + a transverse-profile width.

FromAstronomy
→ Fungia mask→spine→width pipeline giving hyphal centreline, length/branch-count, and diameter once a clean mask exists
SeeAstronomy
SourceKoch & Rosolowsky 2015
Symbols
FWHM
full width at half maximum of the perpendicular intensity profile = spine width
medial axis
1-pixel-wide skeleton (spine) of the mask

3 · Learned segmentation

The data-driven route from a noisy image to a clean hyphal mask, once labels exist — with class-imbalance-aware losses for thin sparse positives and corrective-annotation loops that reach a usable segmenter without a big pre-labelled set.

U-Net encoder–decoder (Dice/Combo loss)

Segment

The U-Net encoder–decoder with skip connections — introduced on a vessel-segmentation benchmark (DRIVE/STARE) — is the default learned route from a noisy image to a clean mask. A Combo (BCE + Dice) loss handles the extreme class imbalance of thin sparse positives against a vast background; a Hough pass can clean the predicted line.

FromVascularAstronomyNeuro
→ Fungiimbalance-aware pixel-wise hyphal segmentation once labels exist; the learned-mask → Hough-cleanup pattern is a strong template
SeeVascular · Astronomy
SourceRonneberger et al. 2015 (U-Net); Jeong et al. 2024 (Combo loss)
Symbols
BCE
binary cross-entropy, the per-pixel classification loss term
Dice
Dice-overlap loss term, robust to thin/sparse-positive class imbalance
Combo
weighted sum BCE + Dice used to train the network

RootNav 2 / SegRoot deep segmentation

Segment

Domain-specialized deep segmenters for occlusion-heavy 2-D projections of branched nets. SegRoot is an encoder–decoder pixel segmenter; RootNav 2 couples a learned segmentation stage to an automatic architecture-recovery step — the same "2-D projection of an overlapping branched net" problem as hyphae on agar.

FromRoots
→ Fungia domain template for a learned hyphal segmentation stage feeding branch_pathfinding architecture recovery on occluded plates
SeePlant roots
SourceYasrab et al. 2019 (RootNav 2.0); Wang et al. 2019 (SegRoot)

RootPainter interactive correction

Segment

Train a CNN on occlusion-heavy images without a big pre-labelled set: the model predicts, a human paints only the fixes on its mistakes, and the model retrains on those corrections — converging on a usable segmenter fast. The operator never labels whole images, only the model's current weaknesses.

FromRoots
→ Fungia GUI corrective-annotation affordance for the ambiguous-junction minority — targets fungal edge precision (~28% in MyceliumSeg), the documented bottleneck
SeePlant roots
SourceSmith et al. 2022 (RootPainter)

MyceliumSeg (fungal-native segmentation)

Segment

A fungal-native segmentation dataset and model — the in-domain reference point for the analog transfers above. It also quantifies the core difficulty: ~84% region accuracy but only ~28% edge accuracy, i.e. the mask body is easy and the hypha boundaries are the hard part. That edge-precision gap is what the corrective-annotation loop exists to close.

FromMycology
→ Fungithe in-domain benchmark and edge-precision target the whole retracing effort is measured against
SeePlant roots
SourceWang et al. 2025 (MyceliumSeg)

4 · Junction resolution & deblending

The accuracy ceiling: in a flat image two strands crossing and one real branch make the identical pixel pattern. Geometry and topology — not pixel degree — decide whether to traverse, branch, or split.

Degree-4 node classification (direction continuity)

Trace

Classify graph nodes by degree, then resolve each degree-4 node by pairing incident edges with the smallest turning angle. Two collinear pairs → an X-crossing (traverse); a single dominant axis with a side branch → a true branch; two strands closing a cycle → an anastomosis. Direction continuity, not degree, decides — the fix for the dominant tracing error.

FromVascularRootsMycology
→ Fungia degree-classification pass after extract_fragment_paths so crossings are traversed, not mis-read as branch points — separates branch vs crossing vs anastomosis
SeeVascular · Plant roots
SourceDirnberger et al. 2015 (NEFI); vascular A/V topology
Symbols
degree-4 node
skeleton-graph vertex with 4 incident edges (node degree = 4)
turning angle
angle between two incident edges; the min-turn pairing tests direction continuity

SExtractor multi-threshold deblending

Deblend

Estimate a background mesh, σ-threshold to connected components, then run a flux multi-thresholding tree that splits blended objects at their saddle points. The classic recipe for separating touching/overlapping sources by relative-brightness topology.

FromAstronomy
→ Fungideblend overlapping/touching hyphae via a multi-threshold tree — best paired with a denoise front-end so it does not fragment on texture
SeeAstronomy
SourceBertin & Arnouts 1996
Symbols
σ
background noise standard deviation; detection threshold is a multiple of σ above the mesh
saddle
flux level between two peaks where a blended blob is split

DRUID persistent-homology deblending

Deblend

Compute a persistent-homology hierarchy of intensity peaks and deblend by topological lifetime: peaks with long birth→death persistence are real sources, short-lived ones are noise. A threshold-free deblender that applies DisPerSE's persistence idea to separating dense overlapping sources.

FromAstronomy
→ Fungithreshold-free topological deblending of dense overlapping hyphae — the deblend counterpart to persistence-based path-quality pruning
SeeAstronomy
SourceWhitehead et al. 2024 (DRUID; arXiv:2410.22508)
Symbols
lifetime
persistence (birth→death) of an intensity peak in the homology hierarchy
merge tree
hierarchy of peaks joined at their saddles; long lifetimes = separate sources

5 · Validation & 3-D (occlusion)

How to know a trace is right — and how to remove the crossing-vs-branch ambiguity at its physical source. Score topology against ground truth, or image in 3-D so overlaps are no longer projected onto one plane.

DIADEM topology-similarity metric

Validate

Score an automated trace against a ground-truth reconstruction by matching bifurcation and termination nodes along the tree — a topology-similarity metric — rather than comparing per-metric summaries. The right QC for a maturing tracer: it rewards getting the branch structure right, not just the totals.

FromNeuro
→ Fungia DIADEM-style topology-similarity diagnostic in _path_quality / _diagnostics as the retracing validation gate before Regime-A metrics ship
SeeNeuroscience
SourceGillette et al. 2011 (DIADEM metric)

X-ray-CT / RSAtrace3D & confocal z-stacks

Validate

Physically remove occlusion by imaging in 3-D. A crossing that is ambiguous in a flat projection is two disjoint strands at different depths in a volume. RSA uses X-ray-CT + RSAtrace3D; the mycology analog is confocal / light-sheet z-stacks — trading throughput for separability.

FromRootsMycology
→ Fungiconfocal / light-sheet z-stacks as the throughput-for-separability trade when 2-D retracing cannot resolve a crossing
SeePlant roots
SourceTeramoto et al. 2020 (RSAtrace3D)

For the PhenoTypic retracing module (branch_pathfinding)

PhenoTypic already runs the right backbone — a multi-source Dijkstra least-cost path with structural-quality path filtering. The literature says the accuracy gains at crossings come from refinements to that backbone, not a different tracing paradigm. From spec §5, the concrete upgrades:

  • Vesselness cost term. Add a Frangi/Sato multiscale-Hessian channel to assemble_composite_cost so faint thin hyphae survive thresholding — the most portable, most cross-validated pre-filter (recommended by three fields at once).
  • Degree-4 node resolution. Classify junctions and resolve degree-4 nodes by direction continuity (smallest turning angle) into crossing vs branch vs anastomosis — the fix for the dominant tracing error.
  • Persistence-based path quality. Rank candidate branches by DisPerSE/DRUID-style persistence (birth→death significance) in _path_quality rather than a raw cost threshold — the noise-robust primitive two unrelated fields converged on.
  • Astronomy denoise front-end. Prepend a starlet/curvelet denoise (strip agar texture while keeping thin hyphae) and a matched/Radon line filter to lift faint hyphae above the per-pixel floor before any thresholding.
  • DIADEM-style validation gate. Add a topology-similarity diagnostic to _path_quality / _diagnostics so Regime-A metrics ship only once retracing accuracy is measured against ground truth.
  • RootPainter-style interactive correction. Offer a GUI corrective-annotation affordance for the ambiguous-junction minority — the realistic path to closing the fungal edge-precision gap.
