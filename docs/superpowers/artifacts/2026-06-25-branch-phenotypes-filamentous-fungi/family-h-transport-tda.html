Transport networks & TDA fingerprint

Once the hyphae are traced, the colony is read as a weighted transport network: edges carry conductances set by cord thickness, and the whole architecture is summarised by flow centrality, cost–efficiency–resilience trade-offs, and an order-invariant topological persistence fingerprint. These summaries hold up where tip- and junction-counting does not.

Edge (cord) conductance

Regime A🔴 A-fragile

Give each hyphal segment a weight equal to how easily fluid moves through it. Treating a cord as a pipe under laminar (Poiseuille) flow, conductance scales with the fourth power of diameter — so doubling cord width carries roughly 16× the flux. This weight is what turns the bare skeleton into a transport network.

Betweenness centrality

Regime A🟡 A-feasible

For every pair of points, find the cheapest weighted path between them; a node or edge's betweenness is the fraction of those shortest paths that pass through it. High-betweenness cords are the colony's load-bearing highways — bottlenecks through which most internal transport is routed.

Transport efficiency & robustness

Regime A🟡 A-feasible

Score the network on the classic transport trade-off: efficiency is delivered flux per unit of building cost, while robustness is how much of the network stays connected after a cord is cut. Cut each edge in turn and track the connected-core fraction; a resilient mycelium reroutes and barely drops.

Mesoscale community structure

Regime A🟡 A-feasible

Sweep a resolution parameter to partition the weighted graph into densely interconnected communities at many scales at once. The resulting multiscale signature — how communities split and merge as resolution changes — clusters colonies by network phenotype without ever counting individual branches.

Network “traits” trade-off

Regime A🟡 A-feasible

Collapse the network into a small set of ecological-strategy traits — building cost, transport efficiency, and resilience — that trade off against one another. A colony's position in this trait space is a composite descriptor of its foraging-vs-conservation strategy.

TMD persistence barcode / image

Regime A🟡 A-feasible

The Topological Morphology Descriptor walks the rooted hyphal tree outward and records, for each branch, its birth (a tip appears) and death (it merges at a bifurcation) on a radial-distance axis. The set of (birth, death) bars is the barcode; binning it gives a fixed-size persistence image — an order-invariant network fingerprint.

Why these summaries survive imperfect tracing of dense colonies

Unlike tip-, junction-, and angle-counting — which fail on the very first mis-traced crossing — the weighted-graph and TDA summaries are comparatively stable under local tracing errors. Betweenness centrality, transport efficiency/robustness, and especially the TMD persistence barcode are averages or topological invariants over the whole network, so a handful of wrong edges nudge them rather than breaking them. That graceful degradation is precisely why this family is advanced as the phenotype layer for dense, overlapping mycelium where per-branch metrics are untrustworthy.

The persistence idea is not unique to biology. The same birth–death persistence primitive underpins DisPerSE in astronomy, which prunes cosmic-web filaments by statistical significance so it won't hallucinate structure from noise — the recurring noise-robust trick across both fields, and the reason persistence-pruned tracing is a natural next step for the retracing module.

Edge conductance is the family's lone 🔴 A-fragile member: the d⁴ weighting amplifies the few-pixel width error of low-res hyphae, so it is the one metric here that does not degrade gracefully.
