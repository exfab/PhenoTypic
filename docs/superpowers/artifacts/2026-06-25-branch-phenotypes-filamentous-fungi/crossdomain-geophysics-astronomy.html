Geophysics, growth physics & astronomy → fungal branches

These fields treat branching as a physical process, not a list of segments. They lead with whole-pattern descriptors (fractal dimension, lacunarity, Strahler/Tokunaga ordering, Minkowski functionals) and with noise-robust extraction (matched filters, persistence, Hessian ridges) — the closest match to faint, dense, overlapping hyphae on textured agar. One unifying principle runs through both halves: integration beats thresholding — accumulate faint signal over scales, lines, or topology before committing to a detection, so SNR grows as √(pixels integrated) instead of being lost per-pixel.

1 · Geomorphology & growth physics

River-network ordering theory and non-equilibrium growth physics provide whole-pattern, process-level descriptors readable straight off a coarse mask — and they are already the operative theory of microbial colony fractal growth (Matsushita & Fujikawa 1990 measured D≈1.73 for a diffusion-limited bacterial colony, the same Laplacian-growth family as DLA). Topological-ordering metrics need a clean tree (🔴 A-fragile); the fractal/Minkowski/lacunarity descriptors survive dense overlap because they never separate a single branch (🟢 B-robust).

Horton–Strahler & Shreve ordering

Regime A🔴 A-fragile

Hack's law

Regime B🟡 A-feasible

Empirical scaling of mainstream length against drainage area, L ∝ A^h with h≈0.57. The exponent encodes basin elongation: it ties a length scale to an area scale, so a deviation from h=½ measures how elongated (vs compact) the growth is.

DLA / DBM / viscous-fingering growth

2 · Astronomy — thin structures in noise

Astronomy is the one analog field whose entire methodology is built around the low-SNR noise problem the biology fields assume away — extracting sub-pixel-faint filaments and streaks from noisy frames. The recurring trick is integration beats thresholding: accumulate faint signal along a line, across scales, or over topology before committing, so a per-pixel-invisible structure becomes a clear peak. This is the front-end (denoise → enhance → confidence-ranked trace) that a faint, dense, overlapping hyphal field on textured agar most needs.

Matched filter / Radon line integration

Regime B🟢 B-robust

Cross-correlate with a template matched to the signal shape (provably optimal SNR in Gaussian noise), or integrate along every possible line with the Radon transform so a faint streak becomes a peak. Both sum the whole feature's flux instead of trusting low-SNR edge pixels.

Rolling Hough Transform (RHT)

Regime B🟢 B-robust

Run a local Hough transform over a disk around every pixel, returning the probability that the pixel belongs to a coherent linear structure plus its orientation. It asks "is there local linear coherence?" not "is this pixel bright?", so faint coherent hyphae survive while incoherent agar texture averages out.

DisPerSE — Morse-persistence skeleton

Regime A🟡 A-feasible

Find filaments as ridge lines (ascending 1-manifolds) between density critical points, then prune by persistence — the density contrast from a feature's birth to its death. Persistence gives each branch a statistical significance rather than a brightness cut, so noise features are removed with confidence.

Best transfers to dense, overlapping, faint hyphae — ranked

From the astronomy companion's assessment against PhenoTypic's real operating point (poorly resolved, dense, overlapping):

  • 1 · Steger unbiased curvilinear detector — subpixel centreline + width at poor resolution, density-tolerant. Top pick for tracing.
  • 2 · Rolling Hough Transform — noise-robust orientation/anisotropy field at low SNR. Top pick for orientation.
  • 3 · Multiscale Hessian ridge (NEXUS+ / Frangi-style) — variable-width tube enhancement before segmentation.
  • 4 · DisPerSE persistence + DRUID topological deblending — a confidence-ranked skeleton and a deblender that won't invent branches from texture.
  • 5 · Starlet/curvelet denoise + matched/Radon line filtering — the SNR-lifting front-end pair.
  • 6 · U-Net with Dice/Combo loss — the learned route once labels exist.

Lower fit (assumption mismatch): Bisous (marked point process), SCMS density ridges, and MST extraction all assume sparse, well-separated tracers — the opposite of dense overlapping hyphae — so they apply only to a sparse high-confidence detection cloud, not the raw field.

Where the fields converge — the concrete upgrades for branch_pathfinding

Reading geophysics, growth physics, and astronomy side by side surfaces three convergences that point straight at the retracing module's accuracy ceiling:

  • Persistence is the noise-robust primitive — independently invented twice. Astronomy's DisPerSE/DRUID prune candidate branches by topological persistence (birth→death significance), and neuroscience's TMD persistence barcodes summarise branching with the same primitive. Two unrelated fields landed on persistence as the way to rank structure over noise — so ranking candidate hyphal branches by persistence (in _path_quality) rather than a raw cost threshold is a doubly-vouched upgrade.
  • Hessian vesselness is recommended by three fields at once. The vascular analog (Frangi/Sato), astronomy (NEXUS+ / Frangi-style multiscale Hessian), and mycology's own pipelines all reach for eigenvalue-ratio tube enhancement. A Frangi/Sato vesselness channel in assemble_composite_cost is the single most portable, most cross-validated pre-filter available.
  • Astronomy's noise front-end supplies what the biology fields assume away. The biology/physics analogs mostly assume clean imaging; astronomy's whole pipeline is denoise → enhance → confidence-ranked trace. Prepending a starlet/curvelet denoise and a matched/Radon line filter — then tracing with a Steger detector and an RHT orientation channel — gives the cost surface a principled low-SNR front-end before any thresholding happens.

These are the concrete engineering corollaries for sdk_/branch_pathfinding/: a vesselness cost term, a persistence-based path-quality filter, and an astronomy-style denoise → line-filter → subpixel-trace front-end.
