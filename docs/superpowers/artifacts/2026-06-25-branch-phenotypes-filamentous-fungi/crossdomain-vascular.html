Vascular networks → fungal branches

Vessel-network image analysis is the most mature 2-D branching-quantification field, and it is the origin of the tube-enhancement filters and junction-classification ideas the fungal retracing module should adopt. Retinal, microvascular, and OCT-angiography pipelines spent two decades on exactly the problem PhenoTypic faces: pull a dense curvilinear net out of a noisy plane, then decide which crossings are real branches.

The four transfers that matter

Before the method-by-method catalog: the vascular ideas with the clearest path into PhenoTypic's retracing module and Regime-B fallbacks. Each is depicted as it actually operates.

(a) Hessian "tubeness" enhancement

pre-filter🟢 origin tech

A faint, noisy tube cross-section is read by a multiscale second-derivative (Hessian) filter, which responds to a bright ridge bounded by two dark flanks and returns a clean enhanced centerline — the single most portable vascular preprocessing step.

Inputgrayscale (pre-threshold)
→ Fungiadd a Frangi/Sato vesselness channel to assemble_composite_cost so faint thin hyphae survive thresholding
SourceFrangi et al. 1998; Sato et al. 1998

(b) Three tortuosity definitions

Regime A🟡 A-feasible

The same wavy strand scored three ways: the Distance-Metric (path ÷ chord), the Inflection-Count Metric (number of bends × DM), and the Sum-of-Angles Metric (integrated turning per unit length).

Inputtraced skeleton (per branch)
→ Fungiper-hypha waviness once retraced — the MEAN_TORTUOSITY member plus DM/ICM/SOAM variants
SourceBullitt et al. 2003; Hart et al. 1999

(c) Resolving a degree-4 node

Regime A🟡 A-feasible

A four-way skeleton node is the accuracy ceiling: by direction-continuity it is an X-crossing (two strands pass through), but the same degree-4 signature can be a true branch or a fusion loop (anastomosis). Geometry, not degree, decides.

Inputskeleton graph + edge geometry
→ Fungidegree-classification pass after extract_fragment_paths — separates branch vs crossing vs anastomosis
SourceDirnberger et al. 2015 (NEFI); vascular A/V topology

(d) Density where tracing fails

Regime B🟢 B-robust

In a confluent strand bed the individual strands cannot be separated, yet vessel-area %, junction density, and box-counting fractal dimension are still computable — because they never separate strands in the first place.

Inputbinary mask / grayscale
→ Fungithe 🟢 COMPLEXITY family — area fraction, mass FD, lacunarity — for mats too dense to retrace
SourceZudaire et al. 2011 (AngioTool); Zahid et al. 2016

Network density

Regime B🟢 B-robust

Three field-level density readouts: vessel-area % (foreground fraction), junction density (branch points per unit area), and end-point count (free terminals). The AngioTool / AngioQuant family made these the standard angiogenesis quantifiers.

Inputmask (+ skeleton for ρ/end-points)
FromVascularMycologyGeomorph
→ Fungicoverage fraction + junction density per area as 🟢 mask-level branchiness proxies (vs fragile tip/node counts)
SourceZudaire et al. 2011 (AngioTool); Niemistö et al. 2005 (AngioQuant)

Calibre & branching laws

Regime A🔴 A-fragile

Vessel diameter from the distance transform, plus the optimality laws: Murray's law (d₀³ = d₁³ + d₂³ at a bifurcation) and the bifurcation ratio Rb (segment counts across branch orders).

Inputskeleton + diameters (graph tree)
FromVascularNeuroMycology
→ Fungicord/rhizomorph calibre classes & Rall/Murray junction exponent — aspirational until retracing + sub-px width are reliable
SourcePatton et al. 2006; Barry & Williams 2015 (AnaMorf)

Fractal dimension & lacunarity

Regime B🟢 B-robust

Box-counting fractal dimension indexes how completely the vascular bed fills the plane; lacunarity measures the gappiness that two beds of equal FD can still differ in. Retinal and OCT-A work added the multifractal spectrum for density heterogeneity across scales.

Inputbinary mask / grayscale
FromVascularMycologyPhysicsNeuro
→ Fungithe core 🟢 COMPLEXITY members — mass FD, lacunarity, multifractal width — for confluent mats
SourceMasters 2004; Zahid et al. 2016; Stošić & Stošić 2006

Hessian "tubeness" filter (origin tech)

pre-filter🟢 most portable

The Frangi / Sato multiscale vesselness filter — the origin technology behind essentially all curvilinear enhancement. At each scale it eigen-decomposes the image Hessian and rewards a bright tube (one small, one large-negative eigenvalue).

Inputgrayscale (pre-segmentation)
FromVascularAstronomyNeuro
→ Fungia Frangi/Sato vesselness term in assemble_composite_cost — the single strongest transfer for recovering faint hyphae
SourceFrangi et al. 1998; Sato et al. 1998

Degree-4 junction classification

Regime A🟡 A-feasible

Classify graph nodes by degree, then decide whether each degree-4 node is a crossing (two vessels overlap), a true branch, or a fusion — using direction continuity, not degree alone. The A/V-topology step that fixes the dominant tracing error.

Inputskeleton graph + edge directions
FromVascularMycology
→ Fungipost-extract_fragment_paths degree-classification so crossings are traversed, not mis-read as branch points
SourceDirnberger et al. 2015 (NEFI); vascular A/V topology

Learned segmentation (U-Net)

Regime B/A🟡 label-gated

The U-Net encoder–decoder with skip connections, introduced on a vessel-segmentation benchmark, is now the default learned route from a noisy image to a clean vessel mask — replacing or seeding the matched-filter front-ends that preceded it.

Inputgrayscale + labels (training)
FromVascularNeuroRoots
→ Fungilearned hyphal segmentation once labels exist (cf. MyceliumSeg); pairs with corrective-annotation loops to beat edge-precision limits

Density-first metrics (trace-free)

Regime B🟢 B-robust

The OCT-angiography lesson: when vessels are too dense to trace, report vessel-density, perfusion-area fraction, and fractal dimension instead of a graph. Tracing-free metrics that degrade gracefully where skeleton-graph counts collapse.

What transfers from vessels to hyphae — and what does not

Vascular image analysis splits cleanly into two halves, and the split is exactly the Regime A / Regime B boundary PhenoTypic already lives on:

  • Skeleton-graph metrics — tip and junction counts, branch order, bifurcation ratio, Murray's law, per-segment diameter — degrade on dense mycelium: overlap creates false nodes, hides tips, and collapses angles. These are the 🟡/🔴 rows, gated on retracing maturity.
  • Whole-field density / fractal / lacunarity metrics — vessel-area %, box-counting FD, lacunarity, multifractal width, mesh area — transfer robustly to confluent mats because they never separate strands. These are the 🟢 ship-now backbone and map onto the new COMPLEXITY family.

The strongest single transfer is the Hessian "tubeness" front-end (Frangi/Sato): a multiscale vesselness channel recovers faint thin hyphae before thresholding and slots straight into branch_pathfinding's composite cost surface — it helps both halves at once.

This is not animal-only machinery. The same vesselness-plus-graph toolkit has a documented port to plant leaf-vein networks, which are likewise a 2-D projection of a dense reticulate branching net — precedent that the vascular methods generalize cleanly beyond the cardiovascular system, and a direct template for applying them to filamentous fungi on agar.
