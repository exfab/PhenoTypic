Solidity

Regime B🟢 B-robust

Ratio of the colony's actual area to its convex-hull area. ≈1 for a compact disc; well below 1 when a filamentous margin leaves large concave voids — a robust compact-vs-diffuse discriminator.

Inputbinary mask
FromMycologyRootsNeuro
SourceTucker & Thomas 1992

Fractal dimension (box-counting)

Regime B🟢 B-robust

Cover the mask with boxes of side ε and count how many are occupied, N(ε). The slope of log N(ε) vs log(1/ε) is the fractal dimension D — an index of space-filling (≈1.2 sparse → ≈2.0 area-filling).

Inputbinary mask
FromMycologyPhysicsVascular
SourceObert 1990; Papagianni 2006
