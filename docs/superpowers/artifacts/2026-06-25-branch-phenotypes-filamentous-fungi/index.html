Phenotype families

Ten families, ordered from the most robust whole-silhouette descriptors down through skeleton-graph topology to dynamics and pigment.

A Size & shape

Area, perimeter, circularity, solidity, eccentricity — classic descriptors from the mask.

Regime B🟢 robust13 metrics B Space-filling & heterogeneity

Fractal dimension, lacunarity, Minkowski, DLA regime — branchiness without separating hyphae.

Regime B🟢 robust12 metrics C Radial / Sholl profiles

Concentric-ring density profiles centred on the inoculum; where the growth front is.

Regime B🟢 robust11 metrics D Texture & anisotropy

GLCM, Fourier, Gabor — branching density & direction from grayscale, no segmentation.

Regime B🟢 robust7 metrics E Margin & macromorphotype

Margin type, pellet/clump/dispersed classification, hairy-length, core/annulus.

Regime B🟡 mixed7 metrics F Network topology

Tips, junctions, branch order, HGU, anastomosis loops — needs a skeleton + graph.

Regime A🔴 retracing19 metrics G Per-branch geometry

Branch angle, internode distance, tortuosity, width & taper — resolution-sensitive.

Regime A🔴 retracing13 metrics H Transport & TDA fingerprint

Conductance, betweenness, transport efficiency, TMD persistence — graceful under error.

Regime A🟡 stable6 metrics I Growth dynamics

Radial growth rate, tip extension, branching rate, autotropism — needs time-lapse.

Dyn🟢/🔴 split15 metrics J Physiology & pigment

Pigment gradients, zonation, sectoring, aerial/substrate — from whole-plate colour.
