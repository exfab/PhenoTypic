Colour & pigment metrics

One card per metric. Definition → how it is read off the colour field (illustration) → input / provenance / source. Most are whole-plate RGB fields; ⏱ marks the one that also needs time-lapse.

Pigmentation intensity

🎨 Phys🟢 B-robust

The colony's average colour: the mean R/G/B or CIELAB L*a*b* taken over every colony pixel. A single colour summary for how dark, saturated, or hued the whole growth is.

InputRGB
FromMycology
SourceHernández-Lauzardo 2018
Output metric
Value
mean R/G/B or L*a*b* — colony-averaged colour over all colony pixels
Units
colour-space value (0–255 per channel, or L*a*b*)
Reads as
low L* / high saturation = dark, deeply-hued colony; high L* / low saturation = pale, washed-out growth

Pigment radial gradient

🎨 Phys🟢 B-robust

How colour or intensity changes from the inoculum outward: sample the colour along a radius from centre to edge and read it as a profile vs radius. Captures dark-core / pale-margin (or the reverse) physiology in one curve.

InputRGB / grayscale
FromMycology
SourceHernández-Lauzardo 2018
Output metric
Value
d(colour)/dr — slope of the colour/intensity profile along the centre-to-edge radius
Units
colour-space value·px⁻¹
Reads as
steep negative = dark core fading to a pale margin; positive = pale centre with a darker rim; flat = uniform hue across the radius
Variables
r
radius — distance along the transect from the inoculum (centre) out to the colony edge

Concentric ring / zonation strength

🎨 Phys🟢 B-robust

How strongly the colony is banded into concentric rings: take the intensity along a radial transect and measure the periodicity and amplitude of its ups-and-downs (e.g. by FFT). High when sharp, regular rings; low when smooth.

Inputgrayscale / RGB series
FromMycology
SourceCesbron 2024
Output metric
Value
Δr, zonation strength — ring period from the dominant radial FFT peak, plus that peak's amplitude
Units
Δr in mm; strength dimensionless (peak amplitude)
Reads as
strong peak = sharp, regularly-spaced concentric banding; weak/absent = smooth, unbanded colour field
Variables
Δr
band spacing — radial period between concentric rings, from the dominant FFT peak of the radial intensity profile

Circadian conidiation banding period

🎨 Phys⏱ Dyn🟢 B-robust

For clock-banding fungi, divide the spacing between conidiation bands by the colony's growth rate to recover a time period (≈24 h in circadian strains). Turns a spatial ring spacing into a biological clock readout.

Inputgrayscale time-series
FromMycology
SourceDunlap & Loros 2007
Output metric
Value
T = Δx ÷ growth-rate — band spacing converted to a temporal banding period
Units
h (≈24 h in circadian strains)
Reads as
evenly-spaced bands at fixed T = regular clock-driven banding pattern; irregular spacing = arrhythmic, uneven zonation
Variables
T
banding period — time between conidiation bands (hours; ≈24 h in circadian strains)
Δx
band spacing — distance between adjacent conidiation bands along the growth axis (mm)
growth-rate
radial extension rate of the colony (mm·h⁻¹) used to convert spacing to time

Sectoring

🎨 Phys🟢 B-robust

Angular wedges of the colony that differ in colour or morphology from the rest — often a mutant or degenerate sub-clone. Reported as the fraction of the colony that is sectored and the number of sectors.

InputRGB
FromMycology
SourceVicente 2025
Output metric
Value
sectored fraction, sector count — Σ wedge angle ÷ 360° and the number of departing wedges
Units
fraction 0–1 (count dimensionless)
Reads as
0 = uniform radial colour; rising = more of the disc split into distinctly-coloured angular wedges
Variables
α, β
individual sectors — angular wedges whose colour/morphology departs from the colony median
Σ wedge angle
total angular span of all sectors; divided by 360° gives the sectored fraction

Aerial vs substrate mycelium

🎨 Phys🟡 lighting-dep.

The split between fluffy, bright aerial hyphae rising off the plate and the dark, flat substrate mat embedded in the agar, read from brightness/texture contrast. Reported as the fraction of the colony that is aerial.

Inputgray / RGB (oblique)
FromMycology
SourceYagüe-type pattern analysis
Output metric
Value
aerial fraction — bright/high-texture (aerial) area over total colony area
Units
fraction 0–1
Reads as
high = fluffy, bright, raised aerial nap dominates; low = dark, flat, substrate-embedded mat

Exudate / guttation droplets

🎨 Phys🟡 lighting-dep.

Surface droplets the colony secretes, seen as bright specular highlights on the mycelium. Counted and measured for number, area, and spatial distribution as a marker of metabolic exudation.

InputRGB (specular)
FromMycology
SourceHernández-Lauzardo 2018
Output metric
Value
droplet count & area — number and summed area of specular-highlight blobs, with their spatial spread
Units
count and area (px²) per colony
Reads as
many bright glints = heavily droplet-studded surface; few/none = clean, un-beaded mycelium

Sporulation density zones

🎨 Phys🟡 calibrated

Local conidia (spore) density mapped across the colony by calibrating gray-value against a measured spore count, then applying that curve pixel-by-pixel. Yields a spatial map of where the colony is sporulating hardest.

Inputgrayscale (calibrated)
FromMycology
SourceLinde 2021
Output metric
Value
conidia density field — per-pixel spore density from the gray-value↔count calibration
Units
conidia·mm⁻² (density map)
Reads as

Melanization

🎨 Phys🟢 B-robust

Darkening of colony zones as melanin accumulates — tracked as a drop in CIELAB L* (lightness), often over time. A direct readout of pigment deposition in defence or stress responses.

InputRGB / grayscale
FromMycology
SourceZapanta et al. 2025 (PUPMCR)
Output metric
Value
ΔL* — drop in CIELAB lightness of a colony zone (magnitude and rate over time)
Units
ΔL* (CIELAB lightness units, over time)
Reads as
large negative ΔL* = zones darkening steeply toward black; near zero = stable, unchanged lightness
Variables
L*
CIELAB lightness of a colony zone; its fall over time tracks melanin accumulation
t₀, t₁, t₂
successive time points at which lightness is sampled

Cheap RGB, colony-scale — but the biology is human-authored

Almost everything in this family comes straight off a whole-plate RGB photo: no branch isolation, no skeleton, no high-resolution microscopy. Pigment intensity, radial gradient, zonation, sectoring, and colour heterogeneity are colony-scale colour fields — they summarise how colour is laid out over the silhouette, so dense and overlapping hyphae never have to be separated. That is exactly why they sit at 🟢 B-robust.

The handful tagged 🟡 lean on acquisition rather than segmentation: aerial-vs-substrate and exudate droplets are lighting/specular dependent; sporulation density needs a gray-value↔count calibration; the colour→biomass/toxin proxy needs a calibration set; and the conidiation banding period additionally needs a growth-rate estimate (the ⏱ flag).

One guardrail: these metrics describe what the colour does, not what it means biologically. Every bio_desc in the schema is left empty for a human domain author — pigment-to-physiology claims are not machine-generated.
