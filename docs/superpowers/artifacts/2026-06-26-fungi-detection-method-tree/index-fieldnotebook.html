Phase congruency & contrast-invariantS2 · baseline

Mechanism: finds features where Fourier components are maximally in phase, giving a dimensionless 0–1 ridge measure that is contrast-invariant — faint hyphae register without per-image intensity tuning. The current detector's front end.

  • Phase congruency (Kovesi)BASEIN REPO
    Log-Gabor quadrature bank → local energy / Σ-amplitude, with a Rayleigh-noise threshold. The reference front end of the baseline detector.
    mathPC = E / (Σn An + ε); local energy E = √[(Σ Ancosφn)² + (Σ Ansinφn)²] over a log-Gabor bank (n scales × m orientations); PC2 adds a noise floor T and phase-deviation weighting W.
    how1 convolve with the log-Gabor quadrature bank → 2 per orientation compute local energy and mean phase, subtract the estimated Rayleigh noise floor T → 3 sum the weighted, phase-deviation-penalized congruency over orientations and normalize by total amplitude → 4 threshold / non-max-suppress the 0–1 map.
    used in Image-quality metrics (FSIM/feature similarity), illumination-invariant edge & corner detection in CV research; PhenoTypic's hyphae front end.

    how1 bandpass-filter at several scales (no orientation sweep) → 2 apply the Riesz transform for the two odd channels → 3 read per-scale amplitude / phase / orientation analytically → 4 accumulate congruency across scales and normalize.
    used in Medical image registration & ultrasound local-phase analysis; rotation-covariant feature extraction.

    how1 map the colour signal into the conformal monogenic framework → 2 compute per-scale conformal-monogenic phase & amplitude jointly across channels → 3 locate PC maxima → 4 threshold; channel-consistent ridges survive, single-channel fringes do not.

Hessian / 2nd-derivative ridge geometryS2

Mechanism: eigenvalues of the Gaussian-smoothed Hessian — a ridge gives one near-zero eigenvalue (along) and one large (across); the eigenvector is orientation. Junction weakness: at a crossing both eigenvalues grow large, so vesselness collapses to ~0.

  • Frangi vesselnessBASEIN REPO
    Blobness × structureness on Hessian eigenvalues, max over σ. The canonical tubularity filter — the control that confirms junction-suppression on your data.
    matheigenvalues |λ₁|≤|λ₂|; V = exp(−Rβ²/2β²)·(1−exp(−S²/2c²)), Rβ=|λ₁|/|λ₂|, S=√(λ₁²+λ₂²); V=0 if λ₂>0 (bright ridge); max over σ.
    how1 smooth at scale σ → 2 build the Hessian and eigendecompose → 3 map the blobness ratio Rβ and structureness S to a [0,1] score → 4 take the max across σ to span hyphal widths → threshold.
    used in Clinical vessel enhancement — retinal fundus, CT/MR angiography, lung & liver vasculature; shipped in ITK / scikit-image.
    variables
    λ₁,λ₂

    Rβ

    S

    β,c
    ratio / structureness sensitivities
    σ
    Gaussian scale (max taken over σ)
    enhance/_focus_edge_frangi.py · skimage.filters.frangi (BSD)
  • Sato tubularityIN REPO

    mathline measure on sorted eigenvalues (λ₁≈0 along; λ_c<0 across) with separate factors α₁,α₂ for the λ_c<0 vs λ_c>0 regimes; scales integrated with per-σ noise equalization.
    how1 Gaussian-smooth → 2 Hessian + eigendecompose, identify the cross-section plane → 3 score "lineness" with asymmetric α-weighting (tolerant of contrast variation) → 4 integrate scales with noise equalization.
    used in 3D vessel, airway & bronchus enhancement in CT; medical visualization (ITK).

    Innovates: a modified eigenvalue tuned for low-SNR, intensity-inhomogeneous filaments.

    Innovates: an eigenvalue ratio (not products) → reinforces bifurcations, single τ knob.
    mathV = λ₂²·(λ_ρ−λ₂)·[3/(λ₂+λ_ρ)]³, with λ_ρ = λ₂ clamped to a fraction τ·max|λ₂|; normalized to [0,1], max over σ.
    how1 multiscale Hessian eigenvalues → 2 regularize the 2nd eigenvalue against τ·max|λ₂| (lifts response at edges/junctions) → 3 compute the ratio-based vesselness → 4 max over scales.

    used in Multiscale vessel & airway segmentation research (neuro / vascular CT).

Cheap classical experiments — training-free, mostly pip

Low-commitment, reversible. These stack with the existing front end (S1 enhancers) or A/B against phase congruency (S2 detectors). Lead with whichever regime the Phase-0 buckets surfaced.

Oriented templates / matched / steerableS1·S2

Mechanism: correlate an oriented template (or synthesise oriented response from a basis) and take the max over orientation. Integrates evidence along the line → the canonical low-SNR booster.

  • Matched filters (Chaudhuri)BASECAVEAT
    Rotated Gaussian-cross-section template bank, max response. The original along-line low-SNR detector; fixed profile blurs junctions.
    mathzero-mean (DC-removed) Gaussian-profile template; bank of K rotations (classically 12 @ 15°); response = maxθ (I ∗ kernelθ), then threshold (MF-FDOG / hysteresis).
    how1 design one Gaussian-profile, zero-mean (DC-removed) template the width of a hypha → 2 rotate it into a 12-orientation bank → 3 correlate each with the image → 4 take the per-pixel max over orientations and threshold (often paired with a first-derivative MF-FDOG gate).
    used in Retinal vessel segmentation (classic DRIVE pipelines); known-profile template detection.

  • Template / normalized cross-correlationPIP · OSS
    Innovates: generalises the matched filter to any elongated template via normalized cross-correlation; no extra deps.
    mathNCC = Σ(f−f̄)(t−t̄) / √[Σ(f−f̄)²·Σ(t−t̄)²] ∈ [−1,1]; slide template, max over rotations.
    how1 pick an elongated reference template → 2 slide it over the image computing normalized cross-correlation at each location (contrast/brightness-invariant) → 3 repeat over template rotations and take the max → 4 threshold the correlation peaks.
    used in Ubiquitous — defect inspection, object localization, particle picking, stereo matching (OpenCV / skimage everywhere).

  • Steerable filters (Freeman–Adelson)CAVEAT

    math1st order: Gθ = cosθ·Gx + sinθ·Gy (higher orders add G2 ridge + Hilbert pair H2); θ-response interpolated analytically; arg-max θ = orientation, quadrature energy = ridge strength.
    how1 convolve once per basis filter (a small fixed set) → 2 interpolate the response at any angle θ in closed form → 3 solve analytically for the orientation maximizing response → 4 take that oriented quadrature energy as ridge strength, threshold.
    used in Orientation & texture analysis, adaptive anisotropic filtering, image pyramids (computational-vision research).

Morphology / path-operators / multiscale transformsS1·S2

Mechanism: select structures by the length of an admissible path through them (not local curvature), or by sparse anisotropic frames. Avoids the local-neighbourhood analysis that breaks Hessian filters at junctions.

    used in Ubiquitous preprocessing — uneven-illumination correction + small-bright-feature extraction (microscopy spot/colony detection, OCR, defect inspection).

Voting / transform accumulatorsS2

Mechanism: accumulate evidence in a parameter space (orientation, ρ–θ) so a faint coherent line beats the per-pixel noise floor — plus statistical false-alarm control.

  • Radon / Hough lineBASEPIP · OSS
    Line integrals / accumulator voting → peaks are lines. The canonical integration operator (too coarse alone for curved hyphae).
    mathR(ρ,θ) = ∬ f·δ(ρ − x cosθ − y sinθ) dx dy (Radon); discrete dual = Hough accumulator voting each edge pixel into its sinusoid ρ = x cosθ + y sinθ; peaks → lines.
    how1 build an edge/feature map → 2 each feature votes its sinusoid into the (ρ,θ) accumulator (or integrate intensity along every line) → 3 find accumulator maxima → 4 back-project the peaks to line segments.
    used in CT reconstruction (Radon is the core operator), lane/line detection (ADAS), barcode & document deskew.
    variables

  • Rolling Hough Transform (RHT)PIP · OSS
    Innovates: windowed Hough on a bitmask → per-pixel orientation of faint diffuse lines below the noise floor, single knob Z.
    mathunsharp-mask → binary bitmask; within a circular window DW run a Hough restricted to ρ=0; record θ iff ≥ Z·DW on-bits lie along θ.
    how1 unsharp-mask (subtract a disk-smoothed copy) and threshold to a binary bitmask → 2 roll a circular window of diameter DW over the image → 3 in each window accumulate a 1-D Hough of orientation at ρ=0 → 4 threshold by the fraction Z to emit a per-pixel orientation field (optionally back-project to a mask).
    used in Radio astronomy — interstellar HI / ISM filament & magnetic-field-orientation mapping (FilFinder / astro pipelines).

  • Line Segment Detector (LSD)PIP · OSS

    mathlevel-line angle field → region-grow same-orientation "line-support regions" → fit rectangle → accept iff NFA = (NM)5/2·γ·B(n,k,p) ≤ ε (B = binomial tail of aligned pixels).
    how1 compute the level-line (gradient-tangent) angle per pixel → 2 region-grow connected pixels whose orientations agree within tolerance into line-support regions → 3 fit a rectangle and count aligned points → 4 keep only segments with NFA ≤ ε (a-contrario validation).
    used in SLAM / visual odometry real-time line detection, document & architectural analysis, calibration (OpenCV ecosystem).

Topology-aware losses & tracing netsS2·S3

Mechanism: penalize topological errors (breaks/merges), not just per-pixel overlap — the learned analog of junction preservation. Drop-in riders on any segmenter.
