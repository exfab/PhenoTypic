Third in the series · after the method tree & the reconnection survey

If you can't label every hypha, how do you know the detector worked?

A dense mycelium has no answer key. No one can hand-trace every strand of an overlapping hyphal network, so there is no pixel-level ground truth to score a segmentation against — and the reconnection methods in the companion survey make this sharper, because a bridged gap is exactly where truth is least knowable. So the question becomes: without labels, what tells you a detection is good rather than a plausible fiction?

It turns out the eight fields that lent the method tree its algorithms all face the same wall — cosmic filaments, neurons, particle tracks, vessels, roads, roots, river networks, cytoskeletal fibers all get reconstructed where no truth exists — and each built reference-free evaluation: ways to judge an extraction by what it must satisfy rather than by a label. This report compiles those metrics, with citations, sorts them into seven families, and closes with a concrete label-free evaluation battery for the hyphae methods.

Four ways a field substitutes for a missing answer key REFERENCE-FREE scored against the data / itself, no labels at all  ·  SEMI-GT sparse expert tracing as a fallible proxy reference  ·  SYNTHETIC-GT simulated data with known truth, used to calibrate  ·  NEEDS-GT classic metric that requires a real label (shown for contrast) The through-line: a truly label-free score never asks "does this match the truth?" — it asks "does this explain the data, survive a perturbation, obey a known law, or agree with an independent method?" Those four questions are the seven families below. Semi-GT (expert tracing) and synthetic-GT (phantoms) are the honest fallbacks every field keeps on hand to calibrate the reference-free scores — not replace them.

Seven families of reference-free evaluation

Every no-ground-truth metric in the eight fields answers one of four questions — does it explain the data · is it significant · is it self-consistent · is it plausible/reproducible/agreed — which split into these seven families. Most fields lean on three or four at once.

A · data-consistency / forward-model fit

Does the mask explain the image?

Render the extraction back into image space (predicted intensity / ridges) and score the residual against the raw data. Structure that doesn't help predict the signal is down-weighted or deleted — no label needed, the image is the reference.

SIFT / COMMIT · MSSIM skeleton-vs-image · SOAX F-function

B · significance / false-alarm control

Is it above the noise?

Rank each candidate by a significance score and keep only what exceeds what noise alone would produce. A principled real-vs-spurious cut set by the data's own noise level, not by a truth catalog.

persistence Nσ · a-contrario NFA · flow-accumulation threshold

C · topological self-consistency

Is the skeleton internally sane?

Score intrinsic topology of the extraction alone — connected-component count, spurious islands, spurious loops, Betti numbers, dangling ends. Fewer broken fragments ≈ better, with nothing to compare to.

component / island count · spurious-loop count · endpoint budget

D · scaling-law / physical plausibility

Does it obey the domain's laws?

Recovered structure must fall in expected physical ranges or obey a domain scaling law (widths, line-mass, Horton/Hack ratios, branch-angle and length distributions). Violations flag over- or under-extraction.

Horton / Hack laws · width & line-mass bounds · trait / growth-model fit

E · stability / reproducibility

Does it survive a perturbation?

Nudge parameters, resample, or augment the input and measure how much the output recurs. Structure that flips with a small change is a parameter artifact; edges that recur across resamples are trusted.

bootstrap traversal-frequency · parameter plateaus · test-time-augmentation consistency

F · cross-method / cross-modality agreement

Do independent views converge?

Run several independent detectors on the same data, or correlate with an independent measurement / modality. Convergent structure is trusted; single-method-only detections are suspect. No view is the truth — the agreement is the signal.

12-method comparison · data-vs-simulation · MRI-vs-CT, biomass correlation

G · known-signal / learned no-reference

Recover a signal you planted.

Exploit a known physics anchor (a mass resonance) or a synthetic phantom with known truth, or train a no-reference quality regressor on degraded masks. The planted signal calibrates the label-free score.

tag-and-probe on resonances · synthetic phantoms · no-reference quality CNN · RCA

Neuroscience — tractography & neuron tracing

SIFT · COMMIT · ACT · test–retest · families A·E·D
no in-vivo truth
Why there's no ground truth

There is no in-vivo geometry for white-matter tracts or neurites. Histology is sparse, ex-vivo, cross-species; phantoms (FiberCup, ISMRM-2015) are simplified proxies. Maier-Hein 2017 quantified the wall: pipelines recover ~90% of valid bundles but emit on average 88±58 invalid bundles — sensitivity is easy, specificity is the problem.

Reference-free metrics

The load-bearing idea is forward-model fit: SIFT / SIFT2 filter or weight streamlines until tractogram density reproduces the measured FOD; COMMIT / COMMIT2 cast the tractogram as a linear forward model of the diffusion signal and zero any streamline that doesn't help predict it. Complemented by test–retest & bootstrap reproducibility and anatomical-plausibility priors (ACT: streamlines must start/end in grey matter). For neuron tracing the DIADEM metric needs GT — inter-annotator agreement is the reference-free substitute.

Named metrics SIFT / SIFT2 fwd-fitCOMMIT / COMMIT2 fwd-fittest–retest / bootstrapACT plausibilityDIADEM

Key sources: Smith+ 2013 (SIFT); Smith+ 2015 (SIFT2); Daducci+ 2015 (COMMIT); Schiavi+ 2020 (COMMIT2); Smith+ 2012 (ACT); Maier-Hein+ 2017 (challenge). See references.

Astronomy — ISM & molecular-cloud filaments

MSSIM goodness · parameter sweeps · width bounds · families A·E·D·F
skeleton undefined
Why there's no ground truth

A molecular cloud has no "true" filament map — the skeleton is a human construct, so nothing to score against. Green+ 2017 show extraction is acutely parameter-sensitive: DisPerSE/FilFinder produce thousands of visibly different skeletons, historically chosen by eye. Synthetic benchmarks with known inputs are the only true-GT setting, used as a proxy.

Reference-free metrics

MSSIM self-consistency (Green+ 2017): compute the mean structural-similarity index between the extracted skeleton and the original intensity image — a scalar goodness-of-fit that objectively ranks the parameter-dependent skeletons and replaces visual selection. Plus parameter-stability (a network that flips under a small persistence/threshold nudge is an artifact), physical-plausibility bounds (radial profile, ~0.1 pc inner width, line-mass vs the ~16 M⊙/pc critical value), and multi-tracer / multi-scale agreement (getsf) — features surviving independent channels are trusted.

Named metrics MSSIM goodness-of-fitparameter-stability sweepwidth / line-mass boundsmulti-tracer agreementgetsf benchmark

Key sources: Green+ 2017 (MSSIM); Koch & Rosolowsky 2015 (FilFinder); Arzoumanian+ 2019 (0.1 pc width); Panopoulou+ 2022 (width caveat); Men'shchikov 2021 (benchmarks). See references.

Cosmology — the cosmic web

persistence Nσ · bootstrap · 12-method · distributions · families B·E·F·D
no true catalog
Why there's no ground truth

There is no observed catalog of "true" filaments for the real universe, so real-data evaluation is inherently reference-free. The only truth is a simulation proxy (N-body runs with a nominal skeleton) — which inherits the simulation's own assumptions and depends on how the "true" skeleton is even defined.

Reference-free metrics

Persistence Nσ significance (DisPerSE): keep only topological pairs whose persistence exceeds what noise of a given amplitude produces — a false-alarm cut, not a catalog match. Stability / bootstrap (T-ReX): resample and build a traversal-frequency map — how often each region is crossed across resamples — so high-frequency edges are the reproducible filaments and frequency is the uncertainty. Cross-method agreement (Libeskind+ 2018): twelve independent classifiers on the same simulation, convergence treated as validity. Physical-plausibility distributions: recovered length/mass/connectivity matched to theoretical expectation.

Named metrics persistence Nσbootstrap traversal-frequency12-method agreementlength / connectivity distributionsN-body proxy skeleton

Key sources: Sousbie 2011 (DisPerSE Nσ); Bonnaire+ 2020 (T-ReX bootstrap); Libeskind+ 2018 (12-method); Galárraga-Espinosa+ 2020 (distributions); Codis+ 2018 (connectivity). See references.

High-energy physics — track reconstruction

tag-and-probe · mass-peak width · split-track · χ²/ndof · families G·A·E·F
no per-track truth on data
Why there's no ground truth

In simulation every hit carries a truth particle-ID, so efficiency and the TrackML score are computable. On real detector data no per-track truth exists — a reconstructed track can't be labelled right or wrong. Evaluation switches to data-driven proxies: known-physics anchors and internal fit consistency, validated by demonstrating data/simulation agreement.

Reference-free metrics

Tag-and-probe on a known resonance (Z→μμ, J/ψ): one "tag" fixes the event, the tracker-independent "probe" measures efficiency directly from data (~99.9% for muons). Invariant-mass-peak width of the Z/J/ψ is a momentum-resolution proxy; the peak position gives the scale. Cosmic split-track: split one cosmic muon into upper/lower halves reconstructed independently — the parameter difference has variance = 2×resolution². Fit self-consistency: normalized χ²/ndof, unbiased hit residuals/pulls, hole fraction — all per-track on data. And the overarching data-vs-simulation distribution comparison.

Named metrics tag-and-probe efficiencymass-peak resolutioncosmic split-trackχ²/ndof · residualsTrackML score

Key sources: CMS 2014 (tag-and-probe, split-track, residuals); ATLAS 2014b (mass resolution); ATLAS 2021 (Run-2 muons); Amrouche+ 2019 (TrackML, needs truth). See references.

Vascular imaging & aerial road networks

topology self-consistency · no-reference CNN · RCA · TTA · families C·G·E
GT-heavy field, thin no-GT
Why there's no ground truth

Honest framing: this field is overwhelmingly GT-driven — Dice, clDice, Betti-error, APLS all need a reference mask/graph. Reference-free evaluation here is genuinely thin, which is itself the lesson: the label-free tools that do exist are the intrinsic and learned ones below.

Reference-free metrics

Topological self-consistency (heuristic): count connected components, spurious islands, spurious holes on the skeleton as an intrinsic signal — fewer disconnected fragments ≈ better — used inside self-training road pipelines. No-reference quality CNN (Galdran+ 2018): degrade expert masks, train a network to regress mask-vs-GT similarity, then predict quality of unseen segmentations with no reference (its heuristics explicitly reward segment connectivity). Reverse Classification Accuracy (Valindria+ 2017): predict per-image Dice with no GT via a reverse classifier + reference atlas. Test-time-augmentation consistency (Wang+ 2019): variance under augmentations/MC-dropout as a label-free failure flag.

Named metrics component / island / loop countTTA consistencyno-reference quality CNNReverse Classification AccuracyclDice · Betti · APLS

Key sources: Galdran+ 2018 (no-reference CNN); Valindria+ 2017 (RCA); Wang+ 2019 (TTA); Yang+ 2017 (unsupervised eval); Shit+ 2021 (clDice, needs GT). See references.

Plant root system architecture (RSA)

trait reproducibility · growth-model fit · cross-modality · families D·F·E
destructive sampling loses truth
Why there's no ground truth

Intact roots in soil can't be labelled without destroying the architecture — excavation severs fine laterals, sectioning loses 3-D topology. The field treats expert manual tracing as a semi-GT proxy: operator-dependent, tedious, and itself carrying inter-operator disagreement, not a gold standard.

Reference-free metrics

Trait reproducibility & biological plausibility: recovered total length, tip/lateral counts, branching-angle and diameter distributions checked against expected physiological ranges and across replicates — consistency substitutes for pixel GT. Growth-model consistency: fit a dynamic RSA/growth model (Leitner) to recovered traits, or recover parameters on synthetic roots (Ziegler's likelihood-free ABC) — a good fit validates without pixel GT. Cross-modality correlation: image traits vs independent MRI/CT, shovelomics biomass, manual counts. Interactive-correction effort: how much editing RootNav needs is a usability quality proxy. Persistent-homology barcodes as representation-robust descriptors.

Named metrics trait reproducibility across replicatesgrowth-model / ABC fitcross-modality correlationpersistent-homology descriptorsmanual-trace agreement · correction effort

Key sources: Pound+ 2013 & Yasrab+ 2019 (RootNav, semi-GT); Leitner+ 2014 (growth-model); Ziegler+ 2019 (ABC); Metzner+ 2015 (MRI-vs-CT); Li+ 2018 (persistent homology). See references.

Geophysics & hydrology — faults & river networks

Horton / Hack laws · threshold sensitivity · inter-interpreter · families D·E·F
subsurface unobservable
Why there's no ground truth

The "true" network is unobservable: the subsurface is imaged only indirectly through reflection amplitudes (no labeled fault set), and basin-scale channel networks are rarely field-mapped. Near-truth is sparse and local — well-log picks, a few field-surveyed channel heads — so quality is judged by internal consistency and physical plausibility.

Reference-free metrics

Scaling-law consistency (the primary hydrology test): extracted networks should obey Horton's laws (near-constant bifurcation/length/area ratios across Strahler orders) and Hack's law (L ∝ A^h, h≈0.5–0.6). Threshold / drainage-density sensitivity: a defensible network is one where density is stable over a plausible support-area band, or the threshold is fixed by channel-initiation physics. Seismic: inter-interpreter agreement (dispersion among independent experts quantifies uncertainty when no truth exists) and geological plausibility (fault continuity, throw consistency). FaultSeg3D supplies synthetic labeled seismic as a proxy — flagged as simulated, not field, truth.

Named metrics Horton / Hack law fitdrainage-density threshold stabilitygeological plausibilityinter-interpreter agreement · well tieFaultSeg3D synthetic

Key sources: Rigon+ 1996 (Hack); Kirchner 1993 (Horton caveat); Montgomery & Dietrich 1992 & Clubb+ 2014 (channel-head threshold); Schaaf & Bond 2019 (inter-interpreter); Wu+ 2019 (FaultSeg3D). See references.

Cytoskeleton & collagen fiber networks

SOAX F-function · SNR plateaus · network stats · temporal · families A·E·D
dense nets can't be traced
Why there's no ground truth

Dense, overlapping real networks (actin, microtubules, collagen) cannot be exhaustively hand-labeled; manual tracing is feasible only for sparse, non-crossing filaments and is annotator-biased (Özdemir & Nikoloski 2023). Every "gold standard" is a proxy — synthetic phantoms with defined truth, or manual tracing on a small subset. This is the closest analog to dense hyphae.

Reference-free metrics

SOAX F-function (Xu+ 2015), the canonical no-GT metric: F = total extracted contour length minus the length of contour lying in low-local-SNR regions — rewards completeness while penalizing traces that stray off genuine intensity ridges; used to auto-select parameters. It is simultaneously a reconstruction-vs-image consistency check. Parameter/SNR-stability plateaus (SIFNE): sweep thresholds and report the size of the stable optimal region — a wide plateau signals a trustworthy extraction (reliability collapses below ~14 PSNR). Network-statistic plausibility: recovered length, persistence length (worm-like-chain), mesh size, nematic order vs polymer-physics expectation. Temporal coherence (TSOAX): consistent topology across time-lapse frames is itself the quality signal.

Named metrics SOAX F-functionparameter / SNR stability plateaunetwork-statistic plausibilitytemporal coherencesparse manual tracing

Key sources: Xu+ 2015 (SOAX F-function); Zhang+ 2017 (SIFNE stability); Xu+ 2019 (TSOAX temporal); Stein+ 2008 (FIRE network stats); Özdemir & Nikoloski 2023 (review). See references.

The recurring answer. No field waits for a label it can't get. Each asks the extraction to satisfy something it can check without truth: (A) explain the raw data (render it back and score the residual), (B) clear a noise-defined significance bar, (C) be topologically self-consistent, (D) obey the domain's laws, (E) survive a perturbation, and (F) agree with an independent method or modality — with (G) planted signals and synthetic phantoms kept only to calibrate the thresholds. The honest caveats recur too: Kirchner showed a plausibility law can be a statistical inevitability (necessary, not sufficient), and Maier-Hein showed self-consistency without a specificity check lets false structure survive. A good battery mixes families so no single blind spot passes.

A label-free evaluation battery for the hyphae methods

Translating the eight fields into a concrete recipe for scoring the reconnection methods in the companion survey — with no hand-labeled mycelium. Each step names the family it comes from and, where it exists, the hook already in the port spec. Mix them: any one alone has a known blind spot.

Family A · forward-model residual

Does the mask explain the image?

Render the reconnected skeleton back to a predicted ridge/intensity map and score the residual against detect_mat. A bridged gap that adds no explanatory power is a false link — the SIFT/COMMIT idea, transplanted.

Family B · significance gate

Did each link clear the noise bar?

Every bridge must pass an a-contrario NFA / persistence threshold set by the image's own noise — already the NFA gate and persistence denoise in the port spec's Tier B. This is the cheapest, most principled first filter.

Family C · topological self-consistency

Count fragments before and after.

Tally connected components, spurious loops, and dangling endpoints on the skeleton pre/post reconnection. Reconnection should reduce component count without inventing loops — a pure-Python check needing zero labels.

Family D · biological plausibility

Do the traits land in range?

Check total hyphal length, tip density, and branching-angle distributions against known mycelial ranges; optionally fit a hyphal growth model (AMF L-System) for consistency. Flags over-connection that inflates length implausibly.

Family E · stability

Does it survive a perturbation?

Bootstrap / jitter parameters or augment the input and keep the traversal-frequency of each edge. Report edges that recur; a bridge that appears only at one parameter setting is an artifact, not a hypha.

Family F · cross-method agreement

Do independent detectors converge?

Run two or more independent enhancers (Jerman vs bowler-hat vs phase-congruency) and trust structure they agree on. Divergent-only branches are candidates for the significance gate to re-examine.

Family G · synthetic calibration

Tune thresholds on planted truth.

Use synthetic hyphae (the load_synth_yeast_plate analog) with known geometry to calibrate every threshold above — the semi/synthetic-GT that sets the dials, never the headline score on real plates.

Where to start. Families B (already specced as the NFA gate) and C (a topology tally) are nearly free and catch the most common failure — noise-born bridges that fragment or over-loop the network. Add A (forward-model residual) as the strongest single discriminator of "explains the image vs plausible fiction," then E (stability) to separate robust structure from parameter artifacts. D and F are the biological and independent-view sanity checks; G calibrates them all. None requires a hand-labeled mycelium — which is the whole point.
