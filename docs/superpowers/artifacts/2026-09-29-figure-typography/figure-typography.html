PhenoTypic Figure Typography

Round 2: the size ladder. Round 1 is settled, so every candidate below uses the same typeface, colors, chrome and panel letters and differs only in text size and the line weights that go with it. Each candidate is drawn three ways, and the layout adapts the way a careful author would: legends move outside a panel when they would cover data, and crowded category labels are angled. The table reports what that costs.

All figures are drawn by matplotlib 3.10.7 from synthetic, fixed-seed data. Sizes are in points at the printed size.

The A4 page around the figure sets its body text at 11 pt and its caption at 9 pt, for comparison.

Colony growth of auxotrophic deletion strains

Deletion of URA3, HIS3 or LEU2 slowed colony expansion on rich medium relative to the parental strain, with the largest delay in leu2Δ (Fig. 1). The edge effect typical of pinned arrays appears in the plate map, with larger colonies in the outer two rows and columns.

Body text on this page is 11 pt and the caption 9 pt. Figure text at the chosen ladder should read comfortably next to both without taking space the data needs.

What each ladder costs

Data area is the share of the figure inside the data panels, so higher leaves more room for data. Collisions count text that overlaps other text, text cut off at the figure edge, and data points hidden under a legend or label, after the layout has adapted. The last two columns give the tick-label size in screen pixels in two viewing situations.

Laptop PDF assumes the A4 page is fitted to a 1280 px-wide viewer window, so 1 pt ≈ 2.15 px. Slide assumes the full-width figure (159.2 mm) is stretched across a 1920 px-wide 16:9 slide, so 1 pt ≈ 4.25 px; a half-width figure placed alone on a slide doubles that. Body text on screen is usually 14 to 16 px for comparison. These are my assumptions for scale, not measurements of a particular device.
