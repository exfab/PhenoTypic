Which typeface?

Only the three bundled families are identical on every OS. A face that is not bundled either has to ship with the package or silently falls back to DejaVu Sans. That fallback is what today's theme does: it asks for IBM Plex Sans.

Which size ladder for an A4 page?

Each ladder sets tick labels, axis labels and panel letters. The table checks each ladder against the text-size ranges journals publish. Those ranges come from search snippets and were not read from the official pages.

LadderNature 5–7Cell 6–8Science 6–9PNAS 6–12Elsevier ≥7PLOS 8–12Springer 8–12

A check means both the smallest text (ticks) and the largest non-panel text (axis labels) fall inside the range. Scaling the figure to a different column width moves every size with it: a 159.2 mm figure enlarged to Nature's 183 mm double column grows by 15%.

A4 proof

Colony growth of auxotrophic deletion strains

Deletion of URA3, HIS3 or LEU2 slowed colony expansion on rich medium relative to the parental strain, with the largest delay in leu2Δ (Fig. 1a,b). Colony area at 48 h showed the edge effect typical of pinned arrays, with larger colonies in the outer two rows and columns (Fig. 1c).

Body text on this proof is set at 11 pt and the caption at 9 pt, the sizes a typical A4 manuscript or thesis uses. Figure text should read as a step below the caption, not as a separate scale.
